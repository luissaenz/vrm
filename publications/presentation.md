I’ve been analyzing why recording short videos often takes me hours and dozens of failed attempts.

I came to a conclusion: **the problem isn’t technical — it’s mental!!!!**

Does the same happen to you?
When we record, we try to do too many things at the same time:

- Speak without stumbling
- Look at the camera lens
- Remember the script
- Control intonation
- Sound natural
- And then edit everything

It’s exhausting. Too many things at once.

---

## The idea behind the app

After exploring different alternatives, I’m developing an app based on a simple principle: **divide and conquer**.

Instead of recording the entire video in one take, the app breaks the script into short fragments (6–12 seconds), and the process works like this:

### 1. Fragment preparation
You read the fragment while seeing yourself on camera.
To start recording, you just say: **"App, record."**
No touching the screen, no moving, no breaking the flow state you just achieved.

### 2. Focused recording
You record only that fragment, focusing on expressiveness and authenticity.
Recording automatically stops after 1.5 seconds of silence.

### 3. Instant validation
You review the fragment:
- If you like it, you say **"next"**
- If not, you say **"re-record"**

### 4. Automatic assembly by the app
The app:
- Trims unnecessary silences
- Generates automatic subtitles
- Levels the audio
- Merges all fragments
- Applies automatic (or custom) editing

The final result is a complete video — **without having to record it in one go.**

Simple. Fast. Fluid.

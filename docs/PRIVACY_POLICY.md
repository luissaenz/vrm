# Privacy Policy for VRM Atomic Camera

**Last Updated: April 18, 2026**

At VRM (Video Recording with AI), we take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy outlines how our mobile application handles data.

## 1. Local-First Data Processing
VRM is designed as a "Local-First" application. This means:
- **Videos and Audio:** All recordings, clips, and final videos are processed and stored locally on your device's internal storage or your photo gallery.
- **Projects and Scripts:** Your ideas, scripts, and project metadata are saved in a local database on your device.
- **No Cloud Uploads:** VRM does NOT automatically upload your videos, audio, or scripts to our servers or any third-party cloud service.

## 2. Hardware and Permissions
To provide its core functionality, VRM requires the following permissions:
- **Camera:** Used strictly to record video clips for your projects.
- **Microphone:** Used to capture audio for your videos and to enable voice commands for the automated teleprompter.
- **Speech Recognition:** Used to synchronize the teleprompter text with your speech. This processing is performed using your device's native speech recognition services (Apple Siri or Google Speech Services).
- **Photo Library / Storage:** Used to save your final exported videos to your device's gallery so you can access and share them.

## 3. Targeted AI Services
When you use AI features to generate scripts:
- Information you explicitly provide (ideas, topics) is sent to a Language Model provider (e.g., OpenAI, Google Gemini) to generate the script.
- No personal identifiers are sent with these requests unless you include them in your prompts.

## 4. Data Security
Since your data is stored locally, its security depends on your device's security. We recommend keeping your device updated and protected with standard security measures (Passcode, FaceID/TouchID).

## 5. Changes to This Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
